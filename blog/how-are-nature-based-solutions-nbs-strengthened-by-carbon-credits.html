How Are Nature-Based Solutions (NbS) Strengthened by Carbon Credits?

Mar 19, 2026·4 min read

In the increasingly narrowing timeline of the global climate crisis, relying solely on technological innovations is not enough. The world's most advanced "carbon capture" technology is nature itself, a flawless product of millions of years of evolution. This reality has made the concept of Nature-based Solutions (NbS) the most important agenda item for the business world and governments in sustainability literature.

However, protecting and restoring nature requires serious financing. This is exactly where carbon credits, which companies use to achieve their "Net Zero" targets, serve as the most powerful economic engine for implementing and scaling Nature-based Solutions. As the Green Gold Foundation, we examine in this guide this inseparable bond between NbS and carbon finance, and how the projects we carry out in the heart of Africa transform into corporate sustainability.

What Are Nature-Based Solutions (NbS)?

Nature-based Solutions (NbS) are actions to protect, sustainably manage, and restore natural or modified ecosystems, which address societal challenges (climate change, food security, water scarcity, etc.) effectively and adaptively, simultaneously providing human well-being and biodiversity benefits.

Protecting a forest against illegal logging (REDD+), reforesting a degraded area, or keeping peatlands wet are the most fundamental examples of NbS. These ecosystems absorb and sequester carbon dioxide (CO2) from the atmosphere, acting as the largest "carbon sinks" that slow down the climate crisis.

How Do Carbon Credits Scale NbS Projects?

The biggest obstacle to protecting nature is often that destroying ecosystems (e.g., converting forests into agricultural land) is seen as more profitable in the short term. Carbon credits reverse this economic equation.

1. Creating the Financial Value of Conservation

Carbon markets assign a financial value to the ecological service (carbon sequestration capacity) that a tree provides whilst "living". When companies purchase carbon credits from these projects to offset the "residual emissions" they cannot zero out in their own operations, protecting nature transforms into a more profitable and sustainable economic model than destroying it.

2. Creating a Shield for Biodiversity

Nature-based carbon projects do not solely focus on greenhouse gases. Thanks to patrols and conservation areas financed by carbon credit revenues, the habitat of tens of thousands of endangered plant and animal species is secured.

3. Empowering Local People and Social Justice

Local communities are always at the centre of NbS projects. Carbon finance creates green employment in project areas, provides education funds for sustainable agricultural practices such as agroforestry, and creates resources for basic infrastructure investments such as clean water and schools.

The Green Gold Foundation and its Strong NbS Approach in the Congo Basin

As the Green Gold Foundation, we believe in the transformative power of Nature-based Solutions and, with our motto "Be the Breath of the Future", we focus on the Congo Basin, one of the world's most critical carbon sinks.

Our mega REDD+ project, which we carry out in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, is one of the most successful examples of carbon credit and NbS integration:

  • 1.3 Million Hectares of Conservation Area: By taking this massive tropical forest ecosystem under protection, we prevent forest loss caused by illegal logging and destruction by 30% annually.
  • Inclusive Public Approach: We manage the project together with the local people. We organise sustainable agriculture workshops, increasing the welfare of the community and removing the economic pressure on the forests.
  • Climate Membership for Companies: Through our sectoral (aviation, logistics, accommodation) programmes such as Green Gold Wings, Wheels, and Stay, we offer businesses the opportunity to make high-quality, transparent carbon credit donations that have passed MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) processes.

In your company's ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) reporting, the support you provide through the Green Gold Foundation projects is registered not only as an emission offset but also as a tangible "Nature-Based Solution" investment made in global biodiversity and social development in Africa.

Summary

  • What are Nature-Based Solutions (NbS)?: They are strategies that protect, manage, and restore natural ecosystems to combat global problems such as climate change (e.g., forest conservation, reforestation).
  • How Do Carbon Credits Support NbS?: They provide the massive financing required for NbS projects to be implemented. By making the cutting of trees economically disadvantageous, they transform the protection of nature into a sustainable business model.
  • The Green Gold Foundation's Role in NbS: The foundation provides a massive Nature-Based Solution by protecting 1.3 million hectares of forest (REDD+ project) in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It aims to halt annual deforestation by 30%.
  • Opportunity for Companies: Whilst achieving their Net Zero targets with the Carbon Credit Donations they provide through the Green Gold Foundation, businesses directly participate in nature-based climate finance that educates local communities and protects biodiversity.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What is the difference between Nature-Based Solutions (NbS) and technological carbon capture?

Technological solutions (for example, giant facilities that filter carbon from the air) solely focus on reducing carbon emissions and are generally highly costly. NbS, on the other hand, absorbs carbon using natural systems such as forests; whilst also protecting biodiversity, regulating the water cycle, and providing economic benefits to local people without creating extra costs (Co-benefits).

2. Does purchasing carbon credits turn NbS projects into a "greenwashing" tool?

No, it does not, if high-standard projects are selected. Before purchasing carbon credits, companies must reduce their internal emissions (Scope 1-2-3) with scientific targets (SBTi) and solely use credits for residual emissions. The Green Gold Foundation's transparent, verifiable Nord-Ubangi project, which centres on the local people, is managed according to the highest quality standards that prevent greenwashing.

3. Why is "local community empowerment" important in NbS projects?

If the people living around a forest do not have a livelihood, they are forced to cut down the forest. NbS projects eliminate this necessity by providing sustainable agriculture training, clean water, and green employment to local people with carbon finance. The local people's ownership of the project ensures the "permanence" of the project.

4. How can my organisation participate in the Green Gold Foundation's NbS projects?

In line with your business's sustainability targets, you can join our Wings, Wheels, or Stay programmes via our website. Following the carbon footprint calculations suitable for your sector, your "Carbon Credit Donation" is directly transferred to our forest conservation activities in Nord-Ubangi.
