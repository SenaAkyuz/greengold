The Role of Business for a Carbon-Free Future: Offset and Beyond

Mar 26, 2026·5 min read

As we approach the irreversible threshold of the climate crisis, the responsibility on the shoulders of the business world, the driving force of the global economy, is greater than ever. It is no longer considered sufficient for companies to merely shrink the carbon footprint of their own operations. The "Target Net Zero" vision calls on businesses to transcend the boundaries of the traditional understanding of sustainability and take an active role in the restoration of the global ecosystem.

In sustainability literature, this new era is defined by the "Offset and Beyond" approach. So, what does it mean to go beyond carbon offsetting, and how can visionary companies lead this transformation? As the Green Gold Foundation, we discuss the role of the business world in a carbon-free future and climate actions beyond the value chain in full detail in this guide.

Why Is Carbon Offsetting Now Just a Beginning?

Traditional corporate climate strategies focused on becoming "carbon neutral" for many years. In this approach, companies would measure their Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions, reduce as much as they could within their internal processes, and balance the remaining, technologically irreducible "residual emissions" by purchasing carbon credits (Offsetting).

Offsetting is an absolutely indispensable bridge on the path to the net zero target. However, today, international regulators and respected authorities like the SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative) emphasise that simply being declared "neutral" by purchasing carbon will not be enough to save the planet. To limit global warming to 1.5°C, the business world must go far beyond its own borders and protect global carbon sinks.

The "Beyond" Concept: Beyond Value Chain Mitigation (BVCM)

The new gold standard in the climate action of the business world is called Beyond Value Chain Mitigation (BVCM).

The "Beyond" approach is when a company provides financing to nature-based projects outside its own value chain, not just to balance its own emissions, but to slow down the global climate crisis, protect biodiversity, and ensure social justice. While companies have embarked on a decades-long journey to decarbonise their own operations by 90%, they must simultaneously take action today to save the rainforests that are facing the threat of extinction.

Lead the Way to "Offset and Beyond" with the Green Gold Foundation

When implementing a "Beyond" strategy, a company achieves the highest impact through high-quality Nature-based Solutions (NbS). The Green Gold Foundation enables the business world to carry this visionary climate finance directly to the Congo Basin, one of the world's most critical ecosystems.

Our REDD+ (Forest Conservation) project carried out in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo goes far beyond an ordinary offset project:

  • Massive Ecological Impact: We protect a tropical forest measuring exactly 1.3 million hectares against illegal logging, halting annual deforestation by 30% and preventing billions of tonnes of carbon from being released into the atmosphere.
  • Social Transformation and Community Empowerment: The "Beyond" approach puts people at the centre. Within the scope of our project, we do not just protect trees; we provide local communities with sustainable agriculture and agroforestry training, and make clean infrastructure investments. Your climate finance transforms directly into a poverty alleviation tool in Africa.
  • Transparent and Measurable Climate Leadership: Our public approach is based on transparency and independent international MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) processes. Free from the risk of "greenwashing", your investments will stand as proof of your climate leadership in the most prestigious ESG reporting.

  • Green Gold Wings: Transform the unavoidable emissions of the aviation sector into forest conservation projects on behalf of your passengers.
  • Green Gold Wheels: Balance the environmental burden of your logistics and transport operations with green development in Africa.

Offset and Beyond Subject Summary for a Carbon-Free Future

  • What is Offset and Beyond?: It is a next-generation climate strategy that refers to companies investing in nature-based projects to protect global ecosystems beyond their own value chains (BVCM - Beyond Value Chain Mitigation), rather than simply balancing their own carbon emissions (offsetting).
  • The Role of the Business World: To assume global climate leadership by reducing internal emissions in line with the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi), while simultaneously financing projects that halt deforestation (REDD+).
  • The Green Gold Foundation's Solution: The Foundation runs a massive REDD+ project protecting 1.3 million hectares of forest in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. By investing in this project, companies directly contribute to the goal of preventing 30% of deforestation annually.
  • Corporate Impact: Carbon credit donations made to the Green Gold Foundation meet companies' "Offset" needs at international standards, while taking their strategies to the "Beyond" level by supporting the socio-economic development (Education, Agriculture) of the local population.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What is the main difference between traditional carbon offsetting and BVCM (Beyond Value Chain Mitigation)?

Offsetting allows you to appear "neutral" on paper by purchasing 1 tonne of external carbon credits for every 1 tonne of carbon your company produces. BVCM (Beyond), on the other hand, goes beyond your company's carbon accounting to strategically and ethically finance the world's critical sink areas (such as the Congo Basin) to contribute to the global net zero target. Today, BVCM is the indicator of true climate leadership.

2. Why should my company support a project in Africa (the Congo Basin) instead of a local project?

The climate crisis is a global problem, and the atmosphere has no borders. The Congo Basin is the world's second-largest carbon sink after the Amazon, and halting deforestation here is the most urgent action for the climate balance of the entire planet. Furthermore, the social impact (poverty alleviation and community empowerment) created by investments in this region adds unmatched value to corporate ESG scores.

3. How do the Green Gold Foundation's programmes (Wings, Wheels, Stay) work?

These programmes are designed to seamlessly integrate carbon offsetting into the customer experiences of different industries (aviation, logistics, tourism). For example, a hotel or airline company can establish a system that calculates the carbon footprint of the service purchased by the customer and automatically transfers this amount to the Green Gold Foundation's conservation fund in Nord-Ubangi.

4. Does investing in the "Beyond" strategy prepare my company for regulations (e.g., CBAM)?

Yes. Companies that go beyond the value chain in climate action, improve their internal processes, and provide carbon credit support through projects that have undergone high-quality MRV processes naturally become the most prepared companies for the European Union's strict transparency and sustainability regulations, such as CSRD and CBAM. Their brand reputations and market competitiveness increase.
