What is the REDD+ Mechanism and Why is it Important for Companies?

Feb 21, 2026·4 min read · Updated Mar 25, 2026

Nowadays, as we feel the effects of the climate crisis more and more deeply, corporate sustainability goals are not just an internal initiative, but also a global necessity. One of the most effective and measurable solutions developed to reduce carbon emissions and restore ecosystems, in line with the 'Net Zero' vision, is the REDD+ mechanism.

But what exactly is REDD+, which is at the heart of sustainability reports around the world, and why is it an indispensable strategic tool for modern companies? In this guide prepared by the Green Gold Foundation, we discuss how the business world can transform itself in harmony with nature and the critical steps at the heart of this transformation.

What is REDD+ and how does it work?

REDD+ is the acronym for 'Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and Forest Degradation.' The '+' sign at the end represents sustainable forest management, biodiversity preservation, and increased forest carbon stocks.

Developed under the United Nations (UN) Framework Convention on Climate Change, this mechanism provides financial incentives for the protection of forests in areas of ecological importance. The basic logic is quite clear: a large part of global carbon emissions is caused by deforestation. REDD+ projects prevent the cutting of trees and the destruction of natural life, ensuring that carbon remains stored in nature. This protective action is converted into verifiable carbon credits in accordance with international standards, generating climate finance.

Why are REDD+ projects so important for businesses?

Participating in REDD+ projects is now an integral part of the business strategies of forward-thinking companies. Here are the main reasons:

1. Achieving 'net zero emissions' targets

Although companies strive to minimise emissions from their operations or supply chains, achieving zero emissions with current technologies is often technically impossible. High-quality carbon credits obtained from REDD+ projects offset these mandatory and unavoidable emissions, enabling companies to successfully achieve their net zero goals.

2. Corporate reputation and ESG performance

Environmental, social and governance (ESG) criteria are now the issues that most attract the attention of investors, regulators and conscious consumers.

The REDD+ mechanism does more than protect trees; it provides local communities in project areas with multidimensional social benefits such as clean water, healthcare, education, and sustainable economic income. Companies that invest in these projects support their brand vision with transparent and tangible social impact.

3. Risk Management and Compliance with New Regulations

New international legal requirements, such as the European Union's Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), are increasing every day. By calculating their carbon footprint and taking responsibility through reliable mechanisms such as REDD+, companies directly minimise the risks of additional taxes and regulations that may arise in their business operations in the future.

Green Gold Foundation: Concrete Impact Focused on REDD+ in Congo

Green Gold Foundation, our vision is a world where forests thrive sustainably, diverse ecosystems are preserved, and healthy communities are supported.

With a mission to create a harmonious relationship between people and nature, our foundation brings the transformative power of the REDD+ mechanism directly to the field with our motto 'Breathe the Future.'

With our project in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of Congo, one of the lungs of the world, we are achieving the following:

  • We protect 1.3 million hectares of forest: We protect this huge forest area of inestimable ecological importance from illegal logging and destruction.
  • We prevent forest loss by 30%: We aim to reduce annual deforestation in the region by 30% each year, preventing millions of tonnes of carbon from being emitted into the atmosphere.
  • We strengthen local communities: our public approach is based on transparency, inclusion and cooperation. We offer training in sustainable agriculture and forestry, increasing the well-being of the local population and placing the community at the centre of the conservation process.
  • We develop integrated initiatives: Through our programmes, such as Green Gold Wings, Wheels and Stay, we bring the culture of sustainability to different sectors.

You can also calculate the carbon footprint of your company or your personal life on our website and participate in our foundation's Carbon Credit Donation initiatives, creating a concrete impact for our future..

REDD+ Mechanism and Its Importance for Companies Summary

A complete summary of the content's key points is presented in bullet points:

  • What is the REDD+ Mechanism?: It is an international framework that aims to reduce carbon emissions and protect forest ecosystems by preventing deforestation and forest degradation. It ensures that carbon in the atmosphere is sequestered naturally.
  • Why is it important for companies? It enables brands to meet their 'Net Zero' commitments, offset their carbon footprint, improve their Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) scores, and comply with international carbon regulations.
  • What does the Green Gold Foundation do? The Foundation has protected 1.3 million hectares of forest area in the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of Congo. This initiative prevents 30% of forest loss per year in the region and empowers the local population economically and socially.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What does REDD+ stand for?

REDD+ is an acronym for 'Reducing Emissions from Deforestation and forest Degradation'. It means reducing greenhouse gas emissions from deforestation and forest degradation. The '+' refers to the sustainable management of forests.

2. What does my company gain by donating carbon credits?

Your company offsets emissions that it cannot eliminate through its own operational processes with carbon credits. This guarantees that your company achieves 'Net Zero' status, enhances its corporate reputation, and complies with sustainability regulations in international trade.

3. Where exactly is the Green Gold Foundation's forest conservation area and how large is it?

At the heart of our projects is the Nord-Ubangi region of the Democratic Republic of Congo (Congo Basin). A total of 1.3 million hectares of forest in this area is being developed and protected by our foundation.

4. How can we get involved with the Green Gold Foundation as an organisation?

Organisations and businesses can join our fight against climate change by contacting us directly via our website; they can make a Carbon Credit Donation or establish long-term strategic partnerships with us in our community initiatives aimed at empowering local communities.
