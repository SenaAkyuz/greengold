Frequently asked

About the marketplace

1 carbon credit = 1 tonne of CO₂e (carbon dioxide equivalent) avoided or removed from the atmosphere. It is verified by an independent standard (VCS, Gold Standard, etc.), and every credit has a unique serial number.
When a credit is retired, it is marked as "retired" in the registry and can never be used by anyone again. This prevents double counting and guarantees the integrity of the carbon claim. Green Gold Foundation retires the credit on your behalf within 24 hours of your payment.
We accept only projects verified by recognized standards such as VCS, Gold Standard, ACR, CAR, Plan Vivo, or Puro.earth. In addition, our internal review committee examines additionality, permanence, and community-benefit criteria. Our rejected-projects list is shared transparently.
The vintage indicates the year in which the emission reduction represented by the credit took place. Newer vintages (the last 1-3 years) are usually priced higher because they are preferred for recency and reporting compliance. Reporting systems such as CDP sometimes do not accept vintages older than 5 years.
Bank transfer (TRY and USD), corporate credit card, and payment via a predefined corporate limit. For purchases over 50.000 USD, deferred payment by invoice is available. All invoices and receipts are delivered digitally to your dashboard.
