CORPORATE PROGRAM

Corporate Climate Membership

GreenGold Corporate Climate Membership is a dedicated sustainability program designed for organizations working to measure, reduce, and offset their carbon footprints with verified, forestry-based carbon credits.

Member companies and their employees gain access to esg.foundationgreengold.org — a digital ESG platform that enables seamless carbon measurement, reporting, and offset management.

Become a Member Access the ESG Platform
THE PROCESS

How It Works

1

Measure

Calculate your annual carbon footprint (Scope 1, Scope 2, and optionally Scope 3).

2

Offset

Offset your emissions with GreenGold's verified forestry carbon credits.

3

Report & Communicate

Access ESG reports, downloadable certificates, and the GreenGold Climate Seal to share your sustainability performance transparently.
