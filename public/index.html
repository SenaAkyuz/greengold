How Green Gold Foundation works

Carbon transformation in three steps

Measure. Reduce. Offset. — an end-to-end journey with Green Gold Foundation.

01

Measure your footprint

Automatically gather your Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions from ERP, invoice, and meter data, and calculate them in line with the GHG Protocol.

02

Reduce emissions

Discover the highest-impact reduction opportunities with AI-powered scenarios. Track your 2030 and 2050 targets with an SBTi-approved roadmap.

03

Offset the rest

Buy VCS and Gold Standard certified projects from our marketplace in one click and generate your offset certificate the same day.
