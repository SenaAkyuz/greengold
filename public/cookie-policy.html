Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Green Gold Foundation uses cookies and similar technologies on this website.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function and provide information to the site owner.

2. Types of cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary cookies: required for the site to function (e.g., remembering your cookie choices). These do not require consent.
  • Analytics / performance cookies: help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it (e.g., Google Analytics). Enabled only with your consent.
  • Functionality and third-party cookies: some features rely on third-party services (e.g., Google Fonts) that may set cookies or process technical data.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies, including a cookie that stores your cookie-consent preference (gg_cookie_consent). These are always active and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies, which help us understand how visitors use the website. These are set only after you give consent and can be withdrawn at any time. When enabled, we use Google Analytics for this purpose.

3. Your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time through your browser settings or by clearing your cookies. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

4. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be on this page.

5. Contact

Questions: info@greengoldfondation.com.
