Onboarding journey

From zero to a live dashboard in 14 days

Our standard onboarding takes less than two weeks. For more comprehensive enterprise projects, we assign a six-week end-to-end implementation team.

Request an Onboarding Plan →

Discovery & Boundary Setting 1-2 days

The operational boundary, reporting period, and target frameworks (CSRD, TSRS, CDP) are defined. The subsidiary structure is modeled.

Data Connections 3-5 days

Automatic data channels are opened from ERPs such as SAP, Oracle, Logo, Netsis, and IFS, from the EPDK energy portal, and from OCR invoice processing.

Calculation & Validation 2-3 days

The first baseline is calculated. Green Gold Foundation AI flags inconsistencies in the data, which you can validate in one click.

Go-Live & Training 2 days

The dashboard is opened to the team, and after a 90-minute training the sustainability unit begins working independently. Live chat is available for additional support.

Frequently asked

About carbon measurement

All calculations comply with the GHG Protocol Corporate Standard and Scope 3 Standard, and are cross-referenced with ISO 14064-1 (Organization-Level Greenhouse Gases). Sector-specific frameworks supported include PCAF (finance), GLEC (logistics), HCMI (hospitality), CORSIA (aviation), and PAS 2050 (product).
We use 95+ sector factors, updated annually, from DEFRA (UK), IPCC, EPA (US), EEA (EU), and national greenhouse gas inventories (including Türkiye's TÜİK). You can also upload your company's specific factors and have them prioritized in calculations.
There are three ways: (1) manual upload via an Excel template, (2) automatic API pull from your ERP/accounting system (SAP, Oracle, Logo, Netsis, Mikro, and more are ready), (3) IoT meters and OCR invoice reading. On the Growth plan, a typical customer receives 72% of their data through automated channels.
Consolidation is defined by choosing operational control, financial control, or equity share. Each subsidiary is modeled as a separate "facility" and roll-up is automatic. Joint ventures (JVs) can be defined with a partial share.
