OUR FOUNDATION

About Us

Our foundation aims to prevent up to 30% of annual forest loss by protecting 1 million hectares of forest in the Nord-Ubangi region of Congo. Protecting these forests is vital for safeguarding biodiversity and mitigating the impacts of the climate crisis. Green Gold Foundation not only protects the environment but also develops sustainable projects that improve the quality of life of local communities.

WHY FORESTS

Why Green Gold Foundation Supports Forests

Forests play a critical role in protecting ecosystems. By storing carbon in the soil and converting atmospheric carbon dioxide into oxygen, they serve a vital function in the fight against climate change. That is why Green Gold Foundation places great importance on protecting the forests of the Congo Basin and supporting sustainable development.

THE CONGO BASIN

The Congo Basin: The World's Largest Tropical Forests

The Congo Basin is home to the world's largest tropical forests after the Amazon. However, due to climate change and human intervention, these forests face the rapid threat of disappearing. Covering a vast area of 3.7 million square kilometers, the Congo Basin — with its expanses of intact forest — is one of the planet's most valuable natural treasures.

OUR MISSION

Let's Work Together for the Future of the DRC

The Congo Basin is one of the largest carbon sinks in the world. Yet its forests continue to fragment and its ecosystems remain under threat. At Green Gold Foundation, we work to protect these unique forests and to promote green economic development by supporting local communities. By protecting forests, we aim to secure not only nature but also the future of people.

OUR VISION

Our Vision

We envision a world where every individual knows their carbon footprint, understands its impact, and has access to ethical and nature-based solutions to balance it.

Our vision is to build a global climate movement where carbon credits are not only an environmental tool but also a means of social justice, renewal, and community development.

PUBLIC APPROACH

Public Approach

Our public approach centers on transparency, inclusivity, and collaboration:

  • Outreach Programs: Hosting workshops and events to educate stakeholders about forestry and sustainable practices.
  • Community Empowerment: Involving local populations in project planning and implementation to ensure sustainable practices align with their needs.
  • Public Awareness Campaigns: Utilizing social media, local media, and partnerships with influencers to amplify our message.
